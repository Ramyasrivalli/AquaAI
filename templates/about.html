About AquaAI Water Intelligence

A production-grade web system designed to evaluate drinking water potability, monitor safety parameters, and deliver automated treatment guidance.

1. Project Objective

Access to safe, clean drinking water is a fundamental human necessity and a critical public health objective globally.

AquaAI combines automated water safety analytics with World Health Organization (WHO) and Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) standards to provide instant potability assessments. By evaluating physical, chemical, and biological parameters, the system empowers municipal plants, water quality inspectors, and consumers to identify contamination risks immediately.

2. Benchmark Dataset

Evaluated against standard water potability datasets containing 3,276 water sample records collected across various water bodies and treatment facilities.

4. Technology Stack
  • Backend Web Framework: Python Flask 3.0
  • Database Engine: SQLite3 with thread-safe context managers
  • Frontend Interface: HTML5, CSS3, Bootstrap 5, FontAwesome 6
  • Data Visualization & Analytics: Chart.js
  • PDF Certificate Generator: ReportLab Engine
  • Inference Classifier: Scikit-Learn Support Vector Classifier (briefly integrated)
5. Key Features
  • Real-time Potability Classification: Instant automated potability assessment.
  • Deterministic Quality Score (0–100): Comprehensive water health rating.
  • WHO/EPA Parameter Diagnostics: Detailed status breakdown against guidelines.
  • Actionable Treatment Guidance: Specific mitigation steps (RO, GAC, Softening).
  • Printable Laboratory PDF Reports: Official certificates with diagnostic logs.
